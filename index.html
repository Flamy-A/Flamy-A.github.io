Research Interest

I am interested in green networking, edge intelligence and computing, the Internet of Things (IoT), mobile crowdsourcing systems, and network intelligence. My research focuses on developing algorithms, protocols, and intelligent systems for sustainable, energy-efficient, and resource-aware networking and computing. I am also interested in applying AI and optimization techniques to industrial IoT, digital twins, smart healthcare, and vehicular edge computing to build scalable and sustainable next-generation networked systems. Building on these interests, I aim to explore autonomous and adaptive AI systems that can reason, formulate hypotheses, conduct experiments, evaluate outcomes, and iteratively improve themselves, with the goal of accelerating scientific discovery and addressing complex real-world challenges efficiently.
